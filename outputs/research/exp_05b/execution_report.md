## Bootstrap preflight and final inference

The earlier 50-draw *support-only* preflight showed that reapplying N≥20 within each draw would exclude 36%, 46%, and 42% of draws for iid, 3-year, and 5-year resampling. Per user approval, N≥20 is now applied only to the original observed grid selection. A separate **50-attempt model-fit preflight** was then run under the approved rule with seeds 20261013/15/17 for block lengths 1/3/5. All 14,400 pooled scenario-slot attempts and all 2,400 primary spatial-slope fits passed. The smoke attempts are implementation diagnostics and were not counted toward final intervals.

Final inference used **1,000 attempted synchronized calendar-year draws** for each of independent years, consecutive 3-year blocks, and consecutive 5-year blocks; seeds were 20261014/16/18 respectively. Calendar blocks are formed from actual consecutive years, never adjacent observed rows across a gap. Every draw is shared across grids, slots, reduced/full models, and paired target/modifier comparisons. Final roster: primary P2 and its P0/P1 baseline, primary Q2 and Q2′ curvature/TV sensitivities, primary grid-specific-additive-slope interaction sensitivity, three alternate modifier P2 runs, and two alternate target P2 runs—no target × modifier factorial expansion. The paired baseline year/grid masks are exactly equal in the current data, verified before fitting.

There are 96,000 pooled scenario-slot attempts per scheme (288,000 total), plus 16,000 primary spatial-slope attempts per scheme (48,000 total). **All were valid**: every interval in `bootstrap_diagnostics.csv` has n_valid=1,000 of n_attempted=1,000; every paired interval in `robustness_comparisons.csv` has n_joint_valid=1,000. No failed draw was retried or silently assigned a zero effect. `bootstrap_final_block{1,3,5}_attempts.csv` retain attempt-level estimates and numerical diagnostics; `bootstrap_smoke_model_preflight_summary.csv` retains preflight validity.

Predeclared numerical checks went beyond rank: local T/V/S condition number ≤10⁸, residual variance fraction for each predictor after the other two >10⁻⁸, raw predictor SD >10⁻¹⁰, pooled/spatial design condition ≤10⁸, finite coefficients with absolute magnitude ≤10⁴ times pooled within-grid Y SD, valid nested R², and at least four repeated rows per grid for local additive identification. Any failure would invalidate the full fixed-roster attempt for that estimand; none occurred. Across final draws the minimum repeated grid count was 5; worst local additive condition was 929.8; minimum T/V/S residual variance fractions were 4.19×10⁻⁶, 4.61×10⁻⁶, and 4.54×10⁻⁶. Worst P2, Q2′, and spatial-interaction conditions were 9.13, 28.90, and 6.32. The largest absolute primary P2 T×S/V×S coefficients were 2.10/1.95 t/ha per standardized product; no extreme-coefficient guard fired. Rare low-row grids remain an uncertainty limitation, even though these checks passed.

Intervals are pointwise 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges of the valid synchronized attempts. They are not familywise simultaneous intervals across 16 slots and multiple models. Baseline targets and weather products are treated as frozen; their construction uncertainty is not in these intervals.

## Main empirical results

Primary pooled P2 VPD dry-minus-wet slope contrast is **negative in 9 of 16 slots and positive in 7**. In the outcome-informed Aug–Nov_(t−1) focal window it is +0.348, −0.300, +0.321, +0.113 t/ha per within-grid VPD SD. The corresponding **three-year-block** pointwise intervals are [−0.112, 0.871], [−1.458, 0.284], [−0.608, 1.025], and [−0.323, 0.525]; all cross zero, as do the independent-year and five-year-block intervals. All 16 primary T and VPD dry-minus-wet slope intervals cross zero under the three- and five-year schemes. Do not turn this into a conclusion of practical equivalence or no interaction; no such margin was approved.

Adding S has modest descriptive overlap: across primary grid-level fits, median incremental R² for M1 over M0 is 0.0126; median absolute change in VPD partial correlation is 0.0173. In Aug–Nov, the median VPD|T,S partials are −0.087, −0.098, −0.063, and −0.080 versus VPD|T −0.104, −0.099, −0.081, and −0.105, respectively. The share of grids with negative VPD|T,S is 71%, 70%, 64%, and 72%. Atmospheric conditional associations are spatially heterogeneous, and the pooled interaction is not a grid-universal response.

Primary P2 joint TS/VS incremental R² over P1 is 0.00181, 0.00053, 0.00087, 0.00025 in Aug–Nov. A nested R² increment is nonnegative by construction; its positive percentile range alone is **not** a test of a coherent interaction direction. Q2, Q2′ (includes T×V), and the grid-specific-additive-slope sensitivity likewise do not produce a stable negative four-month VPD contrast. P50=12 and fixed-composition target point-estimate signs match P50=10 in Aug–Nov; alternate time/depth/rainfall modifiers show additional direction changes, not a consistently stronger-dry pattern. All paired contrasts and their valid-draw intervals are in `robustness_comparisons.csv`.

The pooled primary VPD contrast keeps its sign under leave-one-calendar-year-out in each Aug–Nov slot; maximum absolute changes are 0.156, 0.209, 0.210, and 0.109, respectively. This single-year stability does not overcome the broad block-bootstrap intervals. `conditional_slope_profiles.csv` gives P2/Q2/Q2′ full-derivative evaluations at fixed within-grid mean T and V with local S25/S50/S75, plus support flags. All Aug–Nov grids have T/V range-box overlap across moisture thirds, but a range box does not establish dense joint T/V/S support at the evaluation points.

Descriptive classification: **heterogeneous/mixed conditional pattern, without clear interval-supported moisture modification**. The prespecified negative-dry hypothesis is not consistently supported in this sample; the temperature/VPD/soil-moisture variables should not be kept or dropped on this basis. This remains exploratory, not causal.

Important limits: modern ERA5 soil moisture is a reanalysis proxy, not measured plant-available water, sugarcane stress, or irrigation. Relative grid moisture quantiles are not physiological thresholds. Same annual Y appears in multiple month-slots, shared shocks and neighboring weather limit independent evidence, and Aug–Nov was selected from prior outcome-informed exploration. Conditioning on antecedent moisture changes an observational association; it does not identify a causal pathway. The deeper-layer API label differs from ERA5's physical lower boundary. Neither the success of these numerical checks nor block resampling captures all uncertainty in detrending, data sources, model misspecification, or omitted variables.

No substantive method remains unresolved for this approved EXP-05B execution. Any physiological interpretation, practical-effect margin, variable deletion, or predictive follow-up belongs in a separate Chat-level decision.
